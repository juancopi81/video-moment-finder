Evidence and boundaries
The model behind the playground
A deterministic model of real two-dimensional Euclidean vectors. It computes coordinate products, signed projection and the angle directly. Coordinate controls are limited to −5 through 5; displayed values round to two decimals. This is a generated teaching model, not a measured experiment or a reconstruction of the lecturer’s exact vectors.
The zero-vector case and doubling experiment are generated extensions. Angle is undefined when either vector is zero. Projection onto v is undefined when v is zero. For nonzero v and w, positive scaling preserves the angle.
