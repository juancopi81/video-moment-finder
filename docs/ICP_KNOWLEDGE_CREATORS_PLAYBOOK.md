# ICP Playbook - Knowledge-Heavy Creators

## Hard Recommendation

Primary ICP for the next cycle:

knowledge-heavy creators with owned libraries of long-form educational video, especially:

- course creators,
- coaches,
- consultants,
- niche educators,
- and small training businesses.

This is the best fit for the current product because they already have the raw material, they feel the archive-search pain directly, and they can buy without an enterprise sales process.

## What To Sell

Do not sell "video AI" or "agents."

Sell this outcome:

find the exact teaching, demo, or explanation moment inside your private video archive without scrubbing manually.

## Why This ICP First

- They usually own the source files, so direct upload works with the product as it exists today.
- Their archives are long-form and information-dense, which makes transcript plus visual search more valuable.
- Their problem is repeated, not one-off: repurposing, support answers, course updates, clip sourcing, and objection handling.
- They are reachable through direct outreach, communities, newsletters, and personal demos.
- They can convert through a light-touch or concierge onboarding flow without enterprise procurement.
- This wedge uses the current web product well and leaves agents as an upgrade path for power users.

## Who Not To Chase Right Now

- generic creators looking for viral auto-clipping
- enterprise video-AI platform buyers
- broad API-first developer audiences
- internal search for large organizations with security or procurement requirements

Those segments either expect a broader product surface or a heavier sales and infrastructure story than the current product supports.

## Job To Be Done

When I need to reuse, reference, or repurpose something I already said or showed in a long video, help me find the exact moment instantly.

## Positioning

One-sentence positioning:

Video Moment Finder helps knowledge-heavy creators search their private video archives and instantly find the exact teaching moment they need.

Plain-language promise:

Upload your webinar, lesson, workshop, or demo and search for the exact part where you explain, show, or answer something.

## Best Initial Use Cases

- find every moment where I explain pricing, onboarding, or a common objection
- find the exact lesson or demo section where I show a workflow or dashboard
- find clips to reuse for social posts, FAQs, support replies, or course updates

## Product Wedge Decision

- Primary acquisition wedge: web app with direct upload and search
- Secondary differentiator: agent and API workflows for power users
- Not the lead story right now: generic developer platform positioning

Agents should support the pitch like this:

"Once your archive is indexed, an agent can help you find patterns and moments faster."

Not like this:

"This is an agent product."

## Offer Shape To Test

- Free entry: one video, enough to prove the search moment
- Concierge pilot: manually onboard 3 to 5 videos for early serious users
- Paid test: keep self-serve pricing simple, but be open to selling a manually supported pilot if that closes faster

Do not redesign the full pricing model before you get stronger activation data.

## Outreach Strategy

### Target List

Build a list of 30 prospects across:

- online course creators
- coaches with webinar libraries
- consultants with workshop recordings
- educators with lesson or training archives

Prioritize people who:

- already publish long-form educational content
- clearly reuse or repurpose content
- likely have private archives beyond what is public
- can reply directly without a gatekeeper

### Outreach Message

Use short, concrete outreach. Example:

> I built a tool that lets creators search their own video archive and jump to the exact moment where they explained or showed something. I think it could help with your webinars or course library. If you're open, I'll index one video for you and show you the best queries live.

### CTA

Use only one CTA:

15-minute live demo on one real video.

Avoid asking them to sign up cold before they have seen value.

## Demo Script

For each live session:

1. Ask what kind of archive they have.
2. Ask what they usually need to find later.
3. Upload one real video they care about.
4. Run 5 to 10 queries tied to their real use cases.
5. End by asking whether this would save real time weekly or monthly.
6. Ask what would block them from paying.

## Objections To Track

- I do not have enough archived video yet.
- I already use transcript search.
- I only care about clipping and editing, not search.
- I do not want to upload private files.
- I need this to work on more than one video at a time.
- I do not understand what queries to use.
- The price feels too high before I see repeated value.

## Proof Assets To Build

- one landing-page section for this ICP
- three query examples tied to real educational content
- one short before-and-after demo video
- one case study or testimonial as soon as the first user gets value

## 30-Day Execution Checklist

### Week 1 - List and Message

- [ ] Build a list of 30 target prospects
- [ ] Classify each prospect by sub-segment and content type
- [ ] Draft one outreach message and one follow-up
- [ ] Send the first 10 outreach messages
- [ ] Track source, replies, objections, and meeting status

### Week 2 - Live Demos

- [ ] Run at least 3 live onboarding or discovery sessions
- [ ] Use one real video from each prospect
- [ ] Capture every search query that resonates
- [ ] Capture every moment where the user gets confused
- [ ] Write a short summary after each session

### Week 3 - Positioning and Product Tweaks

- [ ] Rewrite homepage copy for this ICP
- [ ] Add three tailored use cases and search examples
- [ ] Tighten the upload-first onboarding path
- [ ] Improve first-search guidance based on live session notes
- [ ] Add one no-signup demo or proof asset if feasible

### Week 4 - Revenue Test

- [ ] Run at least 3 more sessions, for 6 total or more
- [ ] Ask serious users for a paid pilot or paid self-serve conversion
- [ ] Document the reason for every yes, no, and maybe
- [ ] Decide whether this ICP should stay primary for the next cycle
- [ ] Update the Phase 7 plan with what changed

## Success Criteria

- [ ] At least 10 live sessions completed
- [ ] At least 5 users reach successful search on their own video
- [ ] At least 3 users say the product saves meaningful time
- [ ] At least 3 paid conversions or paid pilot commitments
- [ ] At least 1 testimonial, case study, or strong quote

## Failure Criteria

Reconsider this ICP if most conversations end with one of these:

- they want clipping or editing more than search
- they do not have enough archive pain to care
- they resist upload or privacy constraints strongly
- they understand the value but still will not pay after a live demo

If that happens, move to the next best candidate instead of broadening the product aimlessly.
